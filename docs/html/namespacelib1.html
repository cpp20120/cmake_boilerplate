The result of built-in addition, converted to T.
Precondition
For signed integer arithmetic, the addition must not overflow its promoted operand type.
Note
Integral promotions, conversion to T and floating-point rounding follow the normal C++ arithmetic rules. No overflow or precision checks occur.
